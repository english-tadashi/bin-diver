Privacy

Kataban doesn't require an account and doesn't collect personal information beyond what's described below.

Advertising

Kataban runs Google Ads campaigns and uses Google's conversion tracking tag, which may set cookies to measure ad performance. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Embedded maps

Some blog posts embed Google Maps to show walking routes. Loading these maps sends a request to Google and may set cookies, subject to Google's Privacy Policy linked above.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log IP addresses as part of serving font files.

Affiliate links

Kataban earns a commission on some purchases made through links to Rakuten, Suruga-ya, Buyee (via Indoleads), and eBay (via the eBay Partner Network). These services may set their own tracking cookies when you click through — see each service's own privacy policy for details.
