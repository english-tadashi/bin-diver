Privacy

Kataban doesn't require an account and doesn't collect personal information beyond what's described below.

Analytics

The search tool (the home page) uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and to see what people search for. When you type a search, the text you typed and the number of results are sent to Google Analytics about a second after you stop typing. Google Analytics also records the page address (which can include a search, such as ?q=SLPS-02040), your approximate location, and your device and browser type. Blog posts and this page don't load Google Analytics.

Kataban doesn't send your name, email address, or the contents of My List. Barcodes you scan aren't sent to Google Analytics. Please don't type personal information into the search box.

To opt out, block cookies or scripts from Google Analytics in your browser's settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

Kataban runs Google Ads campaigns and uses Google's conversion tracking tag on the search tool page, which may set cookies to measure ad performance. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Embedded maps

Some blog posts embed Google Maps to show walking routes. Loading these maps sends a request to Google and may set cookies, subject to Google's Privacy Policy linked above.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log IP addresses as part of serving font files.

Product photos and prices

When a search result has a barcode number (JAN) on record, your browser asks Rakuten's product search service about that barcode to show a product photo and the "Used on Rakuten" price guide, and loads the photo from Rakuten's servers. The request contains the barcode number, not anything about you, but like any web request it shows Rakuten your IP address and the page it came from. See Rakuten's privacy policy.

Barcode scanner

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, and the video is processed on your device — nothing is uploaded. On browsers without a built-in barcode reader (such as Safari on iPhone), the scanner loads a reader library from cdn.jsdelivr.net when you open it.

Stored on your device

Kataban saves a few settings in your browser — whether you're shopping in or outside Japan, the blog language you chose, and your My List — plus a copy of the search page so it opens without a signal. These stay on your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Affiliate links

Kataban earns a commission on some purchases made through links to Rakuten, Suruga-ya, Buyee (via Indoleads), eBay (via the eBay Partner Network), and Klook. Nothing is sent to these services until you click a link. They may then set their own tracking cookies — see each service's own privacy policy for details.
