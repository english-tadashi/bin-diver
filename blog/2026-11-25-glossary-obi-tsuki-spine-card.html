What Does “帯付き” Mean? The Obi, or Spine Card, on Japanese Games

2026-11-25

This is the fifth post in our glossary of Japanese used-game terms, after Junk, CIB, Rank B, Unopened and Near-mint and Suruga-ya’s prices. It covers one small piece of paper: the 帯 (obi), and the label 帯付き, “with obi”.

What an obi is

An obi is a strip of paper looped around a product, named after the sash on a kimono and written with the same character. Japanese CDs, LaserDiscs, DVDs and many games came with one folded over the left side of the case. English-speaking collectors usually call the cardboard kind on games a spine card; in Japanese listings it is always 帯.

On games you see it most on disc releases in jewel cases, such as PlayStation and Sega Saturn: it wraps the left edge, so the title can be read on the shelf.

Why it matters

Because it was the first thing many buyers threw away. A copy with its obi still on is harder to find, and collectors pay for that: English Wikipedia notes that products with the obi intact can fetch premium prices. If you want a copy as it was sold, 帯付き is the word to search for.

How it appears in listings

  • 帯付き — the obi is included
  • 帯欠品 — the obi is missing, usually inside a condition note such as (状態：…帯欠品…)

Suruga-ya puts it at the end of the product name and lists those copies separately. On 14 November 2026, a search found, for example, エリア51（帯付き） for the Saturn and パネキット（帯付き） for the PlayStation, and a PS2 Metal Gear Solid collection whose note included 帯欠品.

No label means no promise

This is the part that catches people out. Suruga-ya’s own Q&A says that obi and postcards may not be included with used items, and that as used goods they are basically not treated as part of the item. Shops selling on its Marketplace say the same: one notes that bonus items and obi may be missing from used stock. So if a listing does not say 帯付き, assume there is no obi, even if the photo shows one.

Suruga-ya does define 帯付き as a condition label; our Rank B post covers how its labels work. With a proxy service, quote 帯付き exactly as written in the product name.
