Ura Zelda: Japan’s Master Quest on GameCube and 3DS, the Codes DOL-D43J and CTR-P-AQEJ, and Where the Switch 2 Remake Fits

2026-11-09

  • Nintendo GameCube
  • Nintendo 3DS
  • Action
  • Adventure
  • 2000s
  • 2010s
  • Nintendo

Ura Zelda — 裏ゼルダ, the “other side” of Zelda — is what Japan calls the harder, rearranged version of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time that the rest of the world knows as Master Quest. It was built for the Nintendo 64’s disk drive and never came out there. In Japan it finally appeared on a GameCube disc that was never sold in shops, and later inside the 3DS remake.

Now there is a new Ocarina of Time. Nintendo released a remake for Nintendo Switch 2 on 5 November 2026, and many people are looking at the old versions again. This guide is for collectors who want Ura Zelda as Japan released it: which Japanese discs and cartridges contain it, the codes printed on them, whether they will run on your console, and how to find them.

  • Japanese name: ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナGC 裏 (on the GameCube disc), shortened to 裏ゼルダ
  • GameCube disc: ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナGC, Nintendo, 2002; product ID DOL-D43J-DIS; a free pre-order bonus for The Wind Waker, not sold in shops
  • 3DS cartridge: ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナ 3D, Nintendo, 16 June 2011; product code CTR-P-AQEJ; Ura Zelda opens after you finish the main game
  • Region: the GameCube and the 3DS are both region-locked, so the Japanese copies need a Japanese console
  • Switch 2: the remake came out on 5 November 2026; Nintendo’s product page does not mention Ura Zelda or Master Quest
  • Buying it: search D43J or CTRPAQEJ on Suruga-ya, and 裏ゼルダ ゲームキューブ on Buyee

What is Ura Zelda?

After Ocarina of Time was finished in 1998, Nintendo built a second version for the 64DD, the disk drive add-on for the Nintendo 64. English Wikipedia says it kept the same engine and story but raised the difficulty and changed the dungeons and puzzles. Japanese Wikipedia says the Nintendo 64 cartridge was designed to start this version when a 64DD disk with the extra data was inserted.

The name looks back to the first Legend of Zelda (1986). There, the harder quest you can play after the ending is called 裏ゼルダ in Japan and the Second Quest in English, and both Wikipedias say Ura Zelda followed that idea. 裏 means the back, or the other side.

It was finished, but it never came out on the Nintendo 64. English Wikipedia gives the reasons: the 64DD sold in small numbers, and putting the game on cartridges cost too much. The cheaper discs of the GameCube changed that.

The GameCube disc: a bonus you could not buy

In Japan, Ura Zelda came out on a small GameCube disc called ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナGC. It holds the original Ocarina of Time and ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナGC 裏 together, and it was a pre-order bonus for The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker. According to Game Watch, Nintendo first planned a limited number, then decided on 22 November 2002 to give one to everyone who pre-ordered. Japanese Wikipedia says the discs were handed out with The Wind Waker, which came out on 13 December 2002.

The dates differ by source. Giant Bomb, Chromagi and GameTDB give 28 November 2002, the day Game Watch reported for the start of the pre-order campaign; Japanese Wikipedia gives 13 December 2002. Both are about the same disc.

Its product ID is DOL-D43J-DIS, according to Giant Bomb and Chromagi. The J marks the Japanese disc: Giant Bomb lists DOL-D43E-USA for the North American bonus disc and DOL-D43P-EUR for the European one, both released in 2003 as Ocarina of Time / Master Quest. Sellers do not always write it the same way; one eBay listing we saw wrote DOL-D43J-JPN. Suruga-ya finds the disc under D43J.

One trap on Buyee is ゼルダコレクション (Zelda Collection), a 2004 GameCube disc that Club Nintendo gave as a reward. Japanese Wikipedia says its Ocarina of Time is almost the same as the one on the bonus disc, but we found no source saying whether it includes Ura Zelda, so do not buy it for that. Strategy guides titled 時のオカリナGC裏 コンプリートガイド also appear in the same searches.

The 3DS cartridge: Ura Zelda after the ending

ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナ 3D came out in Japan on 16 June 2011. It is a full remake, developed by Grezzo, and it contains Ura Zelda as well. Game Watch and Japanese Wikipedia both say you can play it after you finish the main game, and that it takes place in a mirrored world. English Wikipedia describes the same: mirrored versions of the Master Quest dungeons and a mirrored overworld.

The Japanese product code is CTR-P-AQEJ, as shown on VGCollect, and Suruga-ya finds the cartridge under CTRPAQEJ. A cheaper reprint labelled ハッピープライスセレクション came out on 15 September 2016, according to Kakaku.com and Suruga-ya. In Buyee searches, many listings have the words 通常版 (standard edition) or ハッピープライスセレクション in the title.

Will a Japanese copy run on your console?

The GameCube disc: only on a Japanese GameCube. English Wikipedia’s page on regional lockout says the GameCube and the Wii are both region-locked, so a Japanese disc will not start on a North American or European GameCube. Our Fire Emblem GameCube and Wii guide covers the same lock.

The 3DS cartridge: only on a Japanese 3DS or 2DS. The same page says the 3DS region lock applies to all software made for it. Our Fire Emblem DS and 3DS guide explains it in more detail.

Both Japanese versions are in Japanese. If you only want to play Master Quest in English, the 2003 Western bonus disc and the Western Ocarina of Time 3D have it. The Japanese copies are for players with a Japanese console and for collectors who want the Japanese release.

Where does the Switch 2 remake fit?

Nintendo released ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナ for Nintendo Switch 2 on 5 November 2026. Nintendo’s Japanese product page gives that date, and Famitsu reported the release on the same day. English and Japanese Wikipedia describe it as a remake of the Nintendo 64 game, with new graphics, voiced cutscenes, a free camera, a jump button and a sprint. It is the second remake, after the 3DS version.

On 9 November 2026, neither Nintendo’s product page nor the two Wikipedia articles listed Ura Zelda or Master Quest for the Switch 2 version. We are not saying it can never be added. But if Ura Zelda is what you want, the Japanese releases that have it are the 2002 GameCube disc and the 2011 3DS cartridge.

Where can you buy it today?

On 9 November 2026 we counted 4 single copies of the GameCube disc among 7 listings for 裏ゼルダ ゲームキューブ on Buyee.

Read each listing before you bid. In the Buyee searches, the GameCube disc appears next to The Wind Waker sets, ゼルダコレクション and strategy guides; the 3DS search also shows posters, soundtracks and guides. On eBay, most Master Quest listings are the 2003 North American or European disc, so look for D43J, Japanese text on the case, or the words 非売品 (not for sale).
