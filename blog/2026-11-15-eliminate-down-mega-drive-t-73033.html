Eliminate Down on Mega Drive: Aprinet’s Only Game, Its Code T-73033, and Why It Never Got a Second Part

2026-11-15

  • Mega Drive (Genesis)
  • Shoot 'em up
  • 1990s
  • Soft Vision

Eliminate Down — エリミネートダウン — is a horizontal shooting game that Soft Vision released in Japan on 25 June 1993 for the Mega Drive, product code T-73033, at 8,500円 before tax. You fly the space fighter Steel Pyron through eight stages against an alien fleet, with three weapons — forward, backward and diagonal — ready from the start.

This is the third entry in a series about Mega Drive games that never became a series. Eliminate Down was the only game its developer, Aprinet, ever made, and there was never an Eliminate Down 2. Thirty years later it did come back, as a new cartridge from Retro-Bit — the same game, made again, which matters when you shop for the original.

  • Japanese title: エリミネートダウン, published by Soft Vision (ソフトビジョン in the Media Arts Database, S・I in Sega’s list), developed by Aprinet
  • Mega Drive cartridge: 25 June 1993 · 8,500円 before tax · T-73033 — the Media Arts Database and Sega’s list of licensee Mega Drive games agree on the date and the price, and Sega’s list gives the code
  • Also in South Korea: a Samsung release, by Hardcore Gaming 101 the same game as the Japanese one
  • No sequel: Aprinet’s only game
  • Reissued: Retro-Bit’s Eliminate Down: Collectors Cartridge, announced in 2023 — a new cartridge, not the 1993 one
  • Buying it: the code at Suruga-ya, and the title plus メガドライブ on Buyee — where the Retro-Bit cartridge shows up too

What is Eliminate Down, and how does it play?

English Wikipedia compares it with Hellfire: the A and C buttons switch between a forward blaster, a rear laser and four-way diagonal bombs, and power-ups raise each weapon through three levels. There are no checkpoints — lose all your lives and the stage starts over — and a shield called the Barrier takes at least two hits. The title screen also has a memory mini-game. Hardcore Gaming 101 calls it an excellent game that pushes the system to its limit, and says it is region-free. English Wikipedia sums up the reviews as mixed. These are the sources’ descriptions; we have not played through the game to check them.

Who made Eliminate Down?

English Wikipedia names Aprinet as developer and Soft Vision as publisher, and Hardcore Gaming 101, Film Stories and Strictly Limited Games all say it was Aprinet’s only game. The Japanese records spell the publisher two ways: the Media Arts Database row has the brand ソフトビジョン (Soft Vision), and Sega’s list of licensee games gives the maker as S・I. We have not found what S・I stands for, so we leave it as Sega wrote it.

Which cartridge is which: the 1993 original or the Retro-Bit reissue?

There are three. The Japanese original is T-73033, from Soft Vision in 1993. Hardcore Gaming 101 says a South Korean version was published by Samsung and is not different from the Japanese one. And in November 2023, Film Stories reported that Retro-Bit would release Eliminate Down: Collectors Cartridge, limited to 4,000 copies, with pre-orders until 2 January 2024 and the cartridge expected in 2024. Strictly Limited Games, which sold it in Europe, lists a coloured “Emerald Nebula” cartridge, a numbered slipcover and a reversible inlay with the original and a new cover, and describes it as the game’s first official release outside Japan and South Korea.

That reversible inlay is why the two get mixed up. On Buyee the Retro-Bit cartridge appears as 復刻版 (“reissue”), often 新品未開封 (“new, sealed”); on eBay as “Retro-Bit”, “Collectors” or “Limited Edition”. A sealed copy of a 1993 cartridge is rare; a sealed Eliminate Down is far more likely to be the reissue. eBay also has a “Reforged” cartridge and a “Chinese product” one, which are neither.

Why was there never an Eliminate Down 2?

We could not find a reason given anywhere, so we will not offer one. What the sources agree on is that Aprinet made this one game and no other. The Media Arts Database has one Eliminate Down row, and neither English nor French Wikipedia mentions a sequel. The 2023 cartridge is the same game reissued, so for this series it does not count as a second part.

Why search Kataban for this one?

Because Kataban lists only the 1993 Japanese release, so the code and the Japanese title you copy from it are the original’s. T-73033, Eliminate Down and エリミネートダウン each return this cartridge and nothing else. In second-hand game shops in Japan, such as Book-Off, games are often shelved in Japanese syllabary order, and this one is under エ.

One developer, one game, and a reissue thirty years on: this cartridge was never followed by a second one. If you want the Japanese original, the code is T-73033, and “Retro-Bit” or 復刻版 in a listing means it is not.
