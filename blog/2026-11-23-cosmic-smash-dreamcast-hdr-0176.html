Cosmic Smash on Dreamcast: Sega’s 2001 Squash-and-Breakout Game, Its Code HDR-0176, and Why It Never Got a Second Part

2026-11-23

  • Dreamcast
  • Sports & racing
  • 2000s
  • Sega

Cosmic Smash — コズミックスマッシュ, printed on the disc as COSMIC SMASH — is a sports game that Sega released in Japan on 13 September 2001 for the Dreamcast, product code HDR-0176, at 2,800円 before tax. English Wikipedia describes it as a futuristic mix of squash and Breakout: you hit a ball at a wall of target tiles in a weightless room and clear them against the clock, riding a line of stations from one stage to the next. It began as an arcade game the same year.

This is the fourth entry in a series about Dreamcast games that never became a series. There was never a Cosmic Smash 2 from Sega. In 2023 other companies released a reimagining for virtual-reality headsets — a new game built on the old one, but not a second part by the company that made it.

  • Japanese title: コズミックスマッシュ (disc: COSMIC SMASH), published by Sega
  • Dreamcast GD-ROM: 13 September 2001 · 2,800円 before tax · HDR-0176 — the Media Arts Database and Sega’s own Dreamcast software list agree on all three
  • The case: one of the few Dreamcast games sold in a DVD-style case, by English Wikipedia
  • Arcade first: Sega’s list links the row to the arcade (NAOMI) version
  • No sequel: C-Smash VRS (2023) is a reimagining by Wolf & Wood Interactive and RapidEyeMovers
  • Buying it: the code at Suruga-ya, and the title plus ドリームキャスト on Buyee

What is Cosmic Smash, and how does it play?

English Wikipedia describes the Dreamcast version. Each stage is a “stop” on a bus or subway line: the first seven, from station 707 to 777, are the same every time, and after that the line forks and you choose left or right at each junction. Clearing a stage adds time to an overall clock. A charged “trick shot” breaks through tiles a normal shot cannot, but the clock runs faster while you charge it. Finish a route with enough trick shots and you meet the only opponent in the game, in a bonus stage. At the end of each run the Dreamcast version shows a code to enter on the official website for a world ranking, a site that no longer exists. There is no Japanese Wikipedia article. These are the sources’ descriptions; we have not played through the game to check them.

Is C-Smash VRS a sequel?

Not in the sense this series uses. English Wikipedia describes C-Smash VRS as a 2023 virtual-reality sports game developed by Wolf & Wood Interactive and published by RapidEyeMovers, a reimagining of Sega’s 2001 game, released first for PlayStation VR2 and later for other headsets and, without VR, for the PlayStation 5. It says the project began with the publisher Jörg Tittel’s fondness for Cosmic Smash and met some resistance at Sega before development started in 2019. A remake by other companies is a new version of the game, not a second Cosmic Smash from the company that made the first.

Why was there never a Cosmic Smash 2?

We could not find a reason given anywhere, so we will not offer one. The Media Arts Database has one Dreamcast row for it, Sega’s list links only to the arcade version, and English Wikipedia mentions no sequel from Sega. English Wikipedia also notes that on home consoles the game was released only in Japan.

Why search Kataban for this one?

Because the shops mix it with other things. At Suruga-ya the title search also brings up the arcade board and its manuals, and on eBay a plain search for Cosmic Smash dreamcast fills up with the American Sega Smash Pack — hence the quotation marks in our eBay link. On Kataban, HDR-0176, Cosmic Smash, COSMIC SMASH and コズミックスマッシュ each return this disc and nothing else. In second-hand game shops in Japan, such as Book-Off, games are often shelved in Japanese syllabary order, and this one is under コ.

A squash game played like Breakout, an arcade game that reached home consoles only in Japan, and a reimagining by other companies twenty-two years later: this disc was never followed by a second one. If you want the Dreamcast original, the code is HDR-0176.
