Gamescom's Persona 4 Revival — And the 1996 PlayStation Game That Started It All

2026-08-27

If you've been following gamescom 2026 in Cologne, you've probably seen the buzz around Persona 4 Revival — Atlus's full remake of the beloved 2008 PlayStation 2 classic. It's one of the most anticipated JRPGs on the show floor this year, bringing the town of Inaba to life with a modern engine and a 360-degree camera.

But before Persona 4, before Persona 3, before Persona 5 made the series a global phenomenon — there was just Persona. Released in Japan in 1996, the original game carried a much longer name: 女神異聞録 ペルソナ BE YOU TRUE MIND (Megami Ibunroku Persona, literally "Revelation of the Goddess: Persona" — commonly shortened to 女神異聞録ペルソナ, or simply Persona). It was the first entry in the Megami Tensei spin-off that would eventually become one of the most beloved RPG franchises in the world.

The Original: 女神異聞録ペルソナ (1996, PlayStation)

This is the game that started it all — years before "Persona" became a household name outside Japan. It never got the same overseas spotlight as its sequels, which makes tracking down a physical Japanese copy a genuinely fun piece of gaming archaeology.

  • Platform: PlayStation (1)
  • Release: September 20, 1996
  • Product code (型番): SLPS00500

That product code is the key to finding it — more on that below.

How to Find It in Our Search

Japanese retro game listings are almost always written in Japanese, and titles don't always translate directly. Our search tool lets you search in English and matches it to the correct Japanese title and product code — no kanji required.

Just search "Persona" and look for 女神異聞録 ペルソナ BE YOU TRUE MIND with the code SLPS00500. That product code is what you'll want to double-check against any listing before you buy — Japanese sellers rely on it far more than the title itself to identify exact versions.

Where to Buy It

A 1996 release is not going to be sitting on a shelf, so secondhand online is the realistic route. Suruga-ya is a Japanese secondhand chain and the only one of the three where the product code alone will find the exact disc. Buyee is a proxy that bids and buys on Yahoo! Auctions and Mercari for you, then forwards the parcel abroad. eBay is the quickest but usually the priciest, because someone has already done the exporting.

Whether Persona 4 Revival brings you into the series for the first time or you've been here since 1996, tracking down the original is a satisfying way to see where it all began.
