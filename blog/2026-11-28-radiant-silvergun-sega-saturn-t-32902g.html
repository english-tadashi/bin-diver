Radiant Silvergun on Sega Saturn: Why the Japan-Only 1998 Disc Is Still the One Collectors Want, and How to Find T-32902G

2026-11-28

  • Sega Saturn
  • Shoot 'em up
  • 1990s
  • ESP
  • Japan Only

Radiant Silvergun — レイディアントシルバーガン — is Treasure’s vertical-scrolling shooter. It started in Japanese arcades in May 1998 and came to the Sega Saturn in Japan on 23 July 1998, published by ESP (English and Japanese Wikipedia; the Media Arts Database).

The game has since reached the West on Xbox 360 and Nintendo Switch, but the Saturn disc was never released outside Japan. If you want the 1998 home version, with the extra story scenes made for the Saturn, the Japanese disc, T-32902G, is the one to buy.

  • The disc: レイディアントシルバーガン, Sega Saturn, ESP, 23 July 1998, 5,800円 before tax
  • Product code: T-32902G; barcode 4534373100020
  • Other versions: arcade (1998, Japan); Xbox 360 download (2011, worldwide); Nintendo Switch (2022, worldwide)
  • Region: the Saturn is region-locked, so a Japanese disc needs a Japanese console or a converter cartridge
  • Buying it: search the product code T-32902G at Suruga-ya and Buyee, and the Japanese title with セガサターン at Buyee as a fallback

What is Radiant Silvergun?

A vertical shooter in which three buttons, alone or together, give you different attacks, and the score you earn powers up the weapons you use (Japanese Wikipedia; English Wikipedia says the weapons stay powered up). Hiroshi Iuchi directed it and Hitoshi Sakimoto wrote the music (English Wikipedia).

The arcade version ran on Sega’s ST-V board from late May 1998 (English and Japanese Wikipedia). For the Saturn, Treasure added animated story scenes made by Gonzo, which the arcade version left out (English Wikipedia, citing the UK Sega Saturn Magazine; Japanese Wikipedia). Japanese Wikipedia also lists an original boss and extra scenario for the Saturn; we found that in one source.

Was it released outside Japan?

Not on the Saturn. English Wikipedia says Western critics imported it despite its region exclusivity, and calls the 2011 Xbox Live Arcade release the game’s first official release in the West; Eurogamer’s 2011 review calls it “the Japan-only Radiant Silvergun”.

The later versions are downloads and modern ports. The Xbox 360 version came out worldwide on 14 September 2011 (English and Japanese Wikipedia); Japanese Wikipedia adds that it refined the graphics and added an Ikaruga mode. The Nintendo Switch version, published by Live Wire, came out on 13 September 2022 in Europe and North America and on 14 September in Japan (English and Japanese Wikipedia). Suruga-ya and Buyee also list Switch packages, including a COLLECTOR’S BOX, so check the platform in any listing.

Why choose the Japanese Saturn disc?

Because it is the original home release, and the only one from 1998. The Gonzo story scenes were made for the Saturn version, and the disc is what collectors look for: English Wikipedia says limited availability made it an expensive, sought-after item, and Eurogamer wrote in 2011 of a “£100+ eBay price tag”. If you only want to play it, the Switch version is the easy way.

How do you play a Japanese Saturn disc?

On a Japanese Saturn, or with help. Sega Retro says the Saturn “is region-locked, meaning that games can only be played on hardware from the same region”, and English Wikipedia’s article on region locking says a switch modification or one of the cartridges that plug into the Saturn’s cartridge slot gets around it; some of those cartridges combine the region bypass with extra memory.

  • A Japanese Saturn: the disc runs as it is
  • A North American or European Saturn: you need a region converter cartridge or a modified console (Sega Retro; English Wikipedia)
  • Language: the bypass does not change the language (English Wikipedia)

Which disc is the original?

T-32902G is the code on Suruga-ya’s trade-in page for the game and in the Redump disc database, and the barcode is 4534373100020 (Suruga-ya). The price was 5,800円 before tax, according to the Media Arts Database; Suruga-ya shows 定価：6,380円, which is that price with today’s 10% consumption tax.

Watch out for three things that share the name: sample discs (Suruga-ya lists one as [サンプル版]), the soundtrack CDs, and the Switch packages.

On 30 October 2026 the product code T-32902G found 2 copies in stock at Suruga-ya and 4 listings on Buyee, and the Japanese title with セガサターン found 25 listings on Buyee.
