Christmas Shipping From Japan in 2026: When to Order From Buyee, Suruga-ya and eBay Sellers, and Which Dates Are Not Out Yet

2026-11-27

  • Shipping & import tax

A retro game bought from Japan as a Christmas present makes two trips: first inside Japan, from the seller to a shop’s or forwarder’s warehouse, then abroad by airmail or courier. The fixed point is Japan Post’s Christmas guideline for international mail. Every shop adds its own days in front of it. This page collects what the shops Kataban links to (Buyee, Suruga-ya and eBay) have published, with the date we checked each page. Where a 2026 date has not been announced yet, we say so and give last year’s value, marked as 2025.

  • Japan Post: Christmas airmail should go out by about early December. The surface-mail guideline (about late October) has already passed.
  • Buyee and Suruga-ya: neither had posted a 2026 year-end schedule on 17 October 2026. Check their notice pages, linked below.
  • Count back: the item has to reach the warehouse in Japan before the international leg can start.

What does Japan Post say about Christmas mail?

Japan Post posts a Christmas notice every autumn. The one dated 14 October 2025, in Japanese and English, put the deadline for delivery by Christmas at approximately early December for airmail and approximately late October for surface mail. It asked senders to post as early as possible, because year-end volume and tight transport capacity can cause delays even to countries with no delay notice. Japan Post’s FAQ gives the same two guidelines. In Japanese, “early December” is 12月上旬, the first ten days of the month.

For 2026, no Christmas notice was on Japan Post’s international mail news page when we checked on 17 October 2026. Until one appears, the guideline above is last year’s value. What 2026 does have are delay notices. On 7 October 2026, Japan Post said that mail to parts of Europe and Africa was still taking longer than usual, because load restrictions linked to the situation in the Middle East had crowded flights and ships in the surrounding regions. Before you choose a shipping method, check Japan Post’s page of service availability by country.

Parcels that contain goods also need customs data, entered through Japan Post’s International Mail My Page service. Japan Post warns that missing or inaccurate details can lead to delays or a return from customs in the destination country. A shop or forwarder fills this in for you, but it is one reason a parcel takes longer than a Christmas card.

Buyee: two stages, and last year’s cut-offs

Buyee buys on your behalf from Japanese sites and forwards the item. Its own notices describe two stages: the seller’s package arrives at the Buyee warehouse and is registered on your My Page, then you complete the shipping procedure and Buyee sends it abroad. Each stage has a year-end cut-off.

On 17 October 2026, Buyee’s Notices page had no 2026 year-end schedule. Last year’s official Buyee announcement, dated 18 December 2025, set these final dates. All are 2025 values, in Japan time:

  • Packages arriving at the warehouse and registered on My Page: until 29 December 2025.
  • Consolidation, protective packaging and photo service: applications until 29 December 2025.
  • EMS, AIR, International ePacket Light, Sea, UPS, DHL and ECMS: the last shipments of the year were for shipping procedures completed by 29 December 2025.
  • Buyee Air Delivery and FedEx: by 25 December 2025.
  • Buyee Air to Taiwan and Buyee Air to Hong Kong: by 23 December 2025.
  • After the break: shipping resumed from 5 January 2026. Packages over 160 cm in total dimensions or 25 kg were not eligible for the last shipment of the year.

Those are the last shipments of the year, not dates that get a parcel to you by Christmas. For Christmas, what counts is Japan Post’s early-December airmail guideline, plus the days your seller in Japan needs to get the item to Buyee’s warehouse. The 2025 notice went up on 18 December 2025. Check the Notices page for the 2026 version.

Suruga-ya: which site you order from matters

Suruga-ya sells through three storefronts. Suruga-ya.JP is the domestic site, and it is the one Kataban’s Suruga-ya links open. Suruga-ya.com is the site for overseas customers, run since 2019. Since January 2025, Suruga-ya has also sold on eBay, with overseas shipping and customer enquiries handled by the BEENOS group, according to the announcements from BEENOS’s BeeCruise and eBay Japan.

Suruga-ya.JP’s shipping guide sets out its own timing. Orders are counted per day, from 8:00 to 7:59 the next morning, Japan time. Paid by card, a PAY service or cash on delivery, an order ships about 1 to 9 days after it is confirmed, and the year-end season and sales can add several days. Bank transfer is paid in advance and takes longer. Yu-Pack parcels arrive 1 to 3 days after dispatch. If you order from Suruga-ya.JP through a proxy or forwarding service, all of that happens before the item reaches the forwarder in Japan.

Suruga-ya.JP’s year-end notice for 2025, dated 26 December 2025, covered payments and phone hours only: bank transfers could take longer to confirm, orders ship after payment is confirmed, and phone support ran from 14:00 to 17:00 between 31 December and 4 January. No 2026 notice was posted on 17 October 2026. We could not load Suruga-ya.com’s pages to check its schedule (the site showed a security check), so look for its notice on the site itself.

eBay sellers in Japan

There is no single eBay deadline. Each seller sets a handling time, which eBay allows from the day of the order up to 30 business days. The estimated delivery range on a listing is calculated by eBay from that handling time, the shipping service, the distance (including international shipping) and when payment clears. Weekends and public holidays are not counted. If you need a game by a certain date, enter your address and choose the shipping method at checkout, then read the estimate before you pay.

Counting back from early December

This is our arithmetic, not a promise from any shop. Japan Post’s airmail guideline is about early December, and the first ten days of December end on 10 December 2026. Suruga-ya.JP’s guide allows up to 9 days to dispatch and 3 days in transit: 12 days before a forwarder in Japan can register the parcel. Counted back from 10 December 2026, that is 28 November 2026. Orders through Buyee depend on how fast each Japanese seller ships. If you are reading this in late November, the order should go in now, not in December.
