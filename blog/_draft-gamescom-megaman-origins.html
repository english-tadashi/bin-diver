Gamescom's Mega Man: Dual Override — And the 1987 Famicom Game Where It All Began

2026-08-29

Capcom made a big splash at gamescom 2026 with Mega Man: Dual Override, the twelfth mainline entry in the classic Mega Man series. It's set to arrive in 2027 — which happens to be the franchise's 40th anniversary, marking four decades since the Blue Bomber's very first outing on the Famicom.

That original game is known in Japan simply as ロックマン (Rockman), released on December 17, 1987 for the Family Computer. It's the game that started everything: the Robot Masters, the weapon-copying mechanic, the whole 8-bit foundation the series still stands on.

The Origin: ロックマン (1987, Famicom)

Here's an honest note for collectors: the original 1987 cartridge doesn't have a documented product code (型番) in either of our reference databases — and that's actually the norm, not the exception. Roughly 70% of Famicom-era titles in the archival record lack one. Sellers list these by title alone, and that's exactly how you'll want to search for it too.

  • Platform: Famicom (FC)
  • Release: December 17, 1987
  • Product code: none on record

Where to buy: Suruga-ya.jp (by Japanese title) · Buyee · eBay
No product code on record, so there is nothing to check a listing against — and a Kataban link cannot narrow this to a single entry. Check the label on the cartridge itself before you buy.

Yes, some of these carry English lettering right on the Japanese box art — "ROCKMAN3" and "ROCKMAN 4" were printed that way originally, so don't be thrown off when you see Latin characters mixed into an otherwise Japanese listing.

How to Find Them in Our Search

Where to Buy

These are all decades-old cartridges, so secondhand is the only route:

  • Suruga-ya — reliable stock of Famicom classics, English-friendly proxy checkout
  • Buyee — proxy service covering Yahoo! Auctions and Mercari, good for rarer listings
  • eBay — convenient if you'd rather skip the proxy step, usually at a premium

As always, watch for condition terms like ジャンク (junk/as-is) on older cartridges — see our glossary guide for what that actually means before you buy.

Whether Dual Override is your first Mega Man or your fortieth anniversary celebration, the 1987 original — and the trilogy that followed it — is where the story really begins.
