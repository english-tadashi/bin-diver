Tsujouban, Genteiban, Shokai Gentei: Japanese Game Editions and How to Tell Them Apart

2026-11-19

通常版 (tsūjōban) is the standard edition of a Japanese game. 限定版 (genteiban) and 初回限定版 (shokai genteiban, “first-print limited edition”) add extras such as a book or stickers. ベスト版 and 廉価版 are cheaper reissues of a game that sold well, and they can carry a different code from the original. Japanese shops list each edition as a separate product, so the word in the listing, or the code, tells you which one you are buying.

  • Standard: 通常版. The game, its case and its manual.
  • Limited: 限定版, 初回限定版, 初回生産版. Same game, plus extras. Check that the extras are actually in the listing.
  • Budget reissue: ベスト版, 廉価版, PlayStation the Best. Same game, cheaper, often a new code.
  • Telling them apart: search the code printed on the spine, on Kataban and in the shop.

What do 通常版, 限定版 and 初回限定 mean?

JapaneseReadingMeaningWhat you get
通常版tsūjōbanStandard editionThe game in its normal packaging
限定版genteibanLimited editionThe same game plus extras, often in a larger box
初回限定版 / 初回生産版shokai genteiban / shokai seisanbanFirst-print (limited) editionExtras included only in the first production run

Japanese Wikipedia’s article on limited editions, written mostly about music releases, describes the same pattern games follow: a limited edition differs from the standard one by special packaging or bonus items, usually costs more, and is most often made for the first production run, which is why the words 初回 (“first time”) and 限定 (“limited”) travel together. What the extras are depends on the product.

One example we have checked against two sources is Magic Knight Rayearth on Sega Saturn. The Media Arts Database lists its 初回限定版 with a fan book, a sticker, a flyer and an outer box, and Japanese Wikipedia describes the same box set with a fan book and stickers. The disc inside is the same game as the 通常版.

Listing titles also use the word 特典 (tokuten). On Buyee on 7 November 2026, Rayearth listings read 初回限定版 ファンブック付き (“first-print limited edition, with fan book”) and 初回限定特典 レイアースファンブック付き, and listings for newer games read 早期購入特典付き. We have not found a source that defines 初回特典 for games, so go by what the title names: if a listing says 限定版 but does not name the extra with 付き (“with”), ask whether it is there.

What do ベスト版 and 廉価版 mean?

廉価版 (renkaban) means “budget edition”. Japanese Wikipedia explains that publishers began re-releasing games at a lower price some time after launch, partly to compete with the used market, and names the lines: Sony’s PlayStation the Best, PlayStation 2 the Best and PSP the Best, Sega’s セガサターンコレクション (Saturn Collection) and Dreamcast Collection. English Wikipedia lists “The Best” as the Japanese counterpart of Sony’s Greatest Hits.

In shop listings, ベスト版 (besutoban, “best edition”) is the everyday word. On 7 November 2026, Suruga-ya’s titles used [ベスト版] and [PS the Best], and Buyee sellers wrote the Best, 廉価版 or 再販 (“reissue”). The game is the same. What changes is the price and, often, the code.

How do Suruga-ya and Buyee list each edition?

Suruga-ya files each edition as its own product, with the edition in brackets. On 7 November 2026, a search for 魔法騎士レイアース listed 魔法騎士レイアース[限定版] and 魔法騎士レイアース [通常版] as two separate items, and the PlayStation Ace Combat, sold in the West as Air Combat, appeared as エースコンバット and エースコンバットベスト. Each has its own price and its own stock.

Buyee’s results are mostly Yahoo! Auctions listings, and the title is whatever the seller typed. The same Rayearth search returned 初回限定版, 通常版 and plain 魔法騎士レイアース with no edition at all, alongside guidebooks and the soundtrack. When a title says nothing, the photos are the only way to tell.

Do different editions have different codes?

Often, yes. The PlayStation Ace Combat came out on 30 June 1995 as SLPS-00061, and its PlayStation the Best reissue followed on 9 August 1996 as SLPS-91005. PSX Data Center lists both, and the Media Arts Database, where Kataban’s data comes from, has a separate row for each.

Limited editions can have their own code too. Magic Knight Rayearth’s limited edition is GS-9018 and its standard edition GS-9058; our Rayearth article gives the database rows and barcodes.

How do you tell editions apart on Kataban?

Search the code. On 7 November 2026, each of these returned one result: SLPS-00061 and SLPS-91005 are the two Ace Combat rows, released in 1995 and 1996, and GS-9018 and GS-9058 are the two Rayearth rows. Kataban does not print the edition name on the result, and both Ace Combat rows carry the Western title Air Combat, so the code is what tells them apart. A title search such as エースコンバット shows both rows together.

Then take the code to the shop. At Suruga-ya we typed it without the hyphen: GS9018, GS9058, SLPS00061 and SLPS91005 each returned only that edition.

Which other Japanese listing words should you know?

Edition is one part of a listing. The condition and contents words have their own guides:

  • ジャンク (junk): sold as-is, not guaranteed to work
  • CIB and 箱説付き: what “complete in box” covers in Japan
  • Suruga-ya ランクB, 未開封 and 美品: what each condition label promises
  • How to buy Japanese retro games without reading Japanese: the rest of the listing words, and how proxies work
