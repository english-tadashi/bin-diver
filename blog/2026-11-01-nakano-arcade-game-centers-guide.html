Nakano Arcades in 2026: namco Is Open, TRF Closed in 2025

2026-11-01

Yes, Nakano has a game center, but not the famous one. Nakano TRF, the fighting-game arcade on the 4th floor of Nakano Broadway, closed on 18 May 2025. The arcade we could confirm as open on 31 October 2026 is namco Nakano, in the Nakano Broadway Annex. If you searched for “Nakano Broadway arcade”, note that the word has two meanings here.

  • “Arcade” as a shopping street: Nakano Broadway and the Sun Mall leading to it are shopping arcades. For the retro game shops, read our Nakano Broadway route.
  • “Arcade” as a game center: this article.

Is Nakano TRF still open?

No. Nakano TRF announced on 2 April 2025 that it and its annex would close, and its last day was 18 May 2025. AUTOMATON and Time Extension both reported it. It opened in October 2004 and lasted more than 20 years.

TRF was known for 2D fighting games and regular tournaments, including Fist of the North Star and Samurai Shodown. AUTOMATON says crowdfunding raised more than 2 million yen twice after the COVID-19 closures, but the finances stayed difficult.

Older guides and map listings may still show it, so do not plan a trip around it. We found no report of a replacement arcade in the same spot.

Time Extension reported that the owner planned to keep the machines and open a new shop in Tsuchiura, Ibaraki Prefecture. Niconico Pedia names it 土浦ビデオゲームE201. That entry is user-written and we could not confirm opening hours, so check the shop’s own channels before travelling.

Which game center is open in Nakano?

On 31 October 2026, Bandai Namco’s official page listed regular hours for namco Nakano, and the Tokubai store directory listed the same hours.

Do not expect a TRF replacement. The official page gives no machine list for this branch, so we cannot tell you what is on the floor today. Its most recent notices were about Eiketsu Taisen in 2022 and crane games in 2019. If you are going for one specific cabinet, call first on 03-5380-5442 or ask a Japanese speaker to do so.

Where should fighting game fans go instead?

Leave Nakano. These two are the names most guides give, but none of the pages we read was official, and their hours disagree, so we give no hours here.

  • Mikado (Takadanobaba): on the 1st and 2nd floors of the Oasis Plaza building, 4-5-10 Takadanobaba, Shinjuku-ku. Guides list a second branch nearby and one in Ikebukuro.
  • HEY (Hirose Entertainment Yard), Akihabara: guides disagree on the opening time, and one lists it as closed on Wednesdays.

Check each arcade’s own social accounts on the day, because game centers do close, as TRF did.

What else can you do at Nakano Broadway?

For most visitors, shopping is the main reason to come. Our route covers the retro game shops, and our Mandarake floor guide shows what is on each floor.

The 1st floor also has XR Center Game Space, a virtual-reality attraction. It is not an arcade, and we did not check its current hours.
