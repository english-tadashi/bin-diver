What Does “中古通常” Mean on Suruga-ya? How to Read the Prices on a Suruga-ya Listing

2026-11-21

This is the fourth post in our glossary of Japanese used-game terms, after Junk, CIB and Rank B, Unopened and Near-mint. Those were about the condition of the game. This one is about the prices around it: a Suruga-ya (駿河屋) listing can show three or four prices at once, and only one of them is what you pay Suruga-ya today.

Our example is the Super Famicom Final Fight Guy (SHVC-FY), as its page looked on 14 November 2026.

中古通常価格: the regular used price, shown during a sale

中古 means used, and 通常価格 means regular price. You only see this label when the item is in a time sale (タイムセール): the regular price is shown crossed out next to the sale price. On the search results that day, our copy read 中古通常価格 ¥16,500, then ￥14,000 税込 — ¥14,000 including tax is the price you pay. The sale has an end time, shown on the listing as a countdown.

Time sales are frequent. A Japanese guide to Suruga-ya’s sales counts them as held almost every day, and the shop’s autumn sale rules said the genres in the time sale changed at 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00 Japan time. If a price looks high, the same copy may be cheaper on another day.

中古 plus a note: which copy you are buying

Suruga-ya lists each condition of a game as its own item. On the product page, the price line starts with 中古 and a short note, and a box labelled その他の状態を選ぶ (choose another condition) lists the other copies. For Final Fight Guy that day:

  • 中古 付属メディア欠け — used, the bundled disc missing: ¥14,000 in the time sale (¥16,500 regular)
  • 中古 箱、説明書状態難 — used, box and manual in poor condition: ¥11,200 in the time sale (¥14,000 regular)
  • 中古 カセットのみ、カセット状態難 — cartridge only, cartridge in poor condition: ¥2,190

So the cheapest Suruga-ya copy was the cartridge alone, at a seventh of the boxed price. Words like 状態難 (poor condition) and のみ (only) are the ones to look for; our Rank B post covers the labels Suruga-ya defines.

定価: the original price, not today’s

定価 is the list price when the game was new: ¥9,350 for Final Fight Guy, in the item details next to the code SHVC-FY. It tells you nothing about today’s value. Our copy also carried a badge, プレミア価格！！ (premium price), and its ¥14,000 was above the ¥9,350 list price. We found no Suruga-ya page that defines the badge, so read it as the shop’s own flag, not a rule.

マケプレ and 他のショップ: other shops selling on Suruga-ya

マケプレ is short for マーケットプレイス, the Suruga-ya Marketplace. On a search result it appears as, for example, マケプレ ￥2,180 (40点の中古品): the lowest price among 40 used copies offered by other shops. On the product page the same thing is a link, 他のショップ (31) 2,180円～ — other shops, from ¥2,180.

Three things differ when you buy from one of them:

  • Who ships it. Suruga-ya’s marketplace guide says that Suruga-ya is the seller, but the shop that listed the item handles customer service and shipping itself.
  • No sale prices. Marketplace items are excluded from time sales, bundle sales and free-shipping campaigns.
  • It may already be sold. Many marketplace shops are real stores that also sell the same copy over the counter (店舗併売品). If it sells in the store first, your order is cancelled.

品切れ and 在庫数: sold out, and how many are left

品切れ means sold out. Search results include sold-out items unless you switch 品切れ to OFF; a sold-out listing can still show a marketplace price, because other shops may have it. On the product page, 在庫数：1 means one copy in stock.

Buying from outside Japan

Suruga-ya ships only within Japan, and that includes the Marketplace. From abroad, you need a proxy or forwarding service; our guide How to Buy Japanese Retro Games Without Reading Japanese walks through it. When you ask a service about a listing, quote the Japanese note exactly, such as カセットのみ, so they buy the copy you mean.
